### How to read this

- **Precision** counts a finding as correct when it matches an in-scope answer-key entry, or when the labeller confirmed it in the app's `labels.json` (correct but not in the answer key, such as a missing header the app's docs do not list). `cannot-tell` counts as incorrect. Unlabelled findings are left out and counted.
- **Actionable precision** (level 2) counts only correct findings the owner should act on: a test fixture's password is correct but not actionable.
- **Strict precision** counts only answer-key matches as correct: a lower bound.
- **Recall** is over root causes (answer-key `group`s) in scope for the run. Entries that need a session or payloads count only in runs that have them.
- The labels here are one provisional labeller's (see each `labels.json`); the protocol needs two, blinded, with κ ≥ 0.70.
