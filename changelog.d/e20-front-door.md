section: Docs

- **E20 draft protocol: how safe is the web's front door?** A passive check
  of the Tranco top 1000 and of about 100 recent Y Combinator startups (E20b),
  with the live check's own rules, published as aggregates only. Not
  registered yet, and nothing has been sent to any site. The runner,
  `bench/front-door/run.mjs`, works on loopback only until the protocol is
  registered and the list file matches the hash recorded in it
  (`docs/research/prereg/E20-front-door.md`).
- **The live check's passive report takes study options:** skip referenced
  files, skip the secret rules, its own User-Agent and robots.txt name. The
  live check itself is unchanged.
