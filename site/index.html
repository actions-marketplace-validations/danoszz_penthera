    Nothing leaves your browser

    This page is served with a Content-Security-Policy of connect-src 'none'. Once it has loaded, it cannot send a request, so your files cannot be uploaded. Open your browser's network tab, run a check and see. There are no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts.

    What this check cannot do

    • Read your dependencies' code. It sees the install scripts package.json declares, not what is in node_modules.
    • Read your lockfile, so version ranges are not matched against known-malicious versions.
    • Scan a running app or a URL.
    • Fix anything, or write a report.

    The full scan does all of that on your machine, offline too: npx penthera.

    Get it

    The guided start. Penthera detects your setup offline, asks what to check, and checks your code, dependencies and AI tooling. It scans your running app only after you confirm the app is yours. Nothing is committed or pushed.

    npx penthera deps
    What the packages in this project can do: shell, network, install scripts.
    npx penthera agents --home
    Audit your Claude Code, Cursor and MCP setup.
    npx penthera ui

    What it does not do

    It finds known classes of problems, not novel ones: misconfigured headers, leaked keys, risky dependency behavior, unsafe agent setups, common injection patterns. It does not find business-logic flaws, complex authorization bugs or zero-days, and it does not replace a human penetration test.

    Its accuracy is measured, on a small sample: precision {{BENCH_P}} (95% CI {{BENCH_P_CI}}) and recall {{BENCH_R}} (95% CI {{BENCH_R_CI}}), on {{BENCH_N}} findings in labs we wrote ourselves. These numbers catch regressions; they do not measure real-world accuracy.

    Open R&D

    Penthera is a research project with a tool you can use today. Each check is measured in public, pre-registered experiments, and the results are published, good or bad. What holds up ships in the next release. What does not is dropped.
