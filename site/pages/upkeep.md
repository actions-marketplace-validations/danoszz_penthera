# Penthera Upkeep: security upkeep for the apps you ship

> In validation. Nothing to buy yet.

Shipping is fast now. Keeping an app safe after launch is the part nobody has time for: new advisories, a dependency that turns bad, a header that slips in a redesign. Upkeep is Penthera doing that work for you, every week, and showing you what it did.

{{CTA}}

## What you would get

- **Scheduled checks** of your site, repository, dependencies and AI agent setup, with the same rules as the free command-line tool.
- **Fix pull requests that a person approves.** Nothing is merged or deployed without you.
- **A monthly evidence report** you can forward to a client, or use to answer a supplier security questionnaire. For teams that ship installable software, it helps collect the evidence the EU Cyber Resilience Act asks for.

## Prices we are testing

- **Team:** one product team, $100 a month.
- **Agency:** several client apps, $300 a month.

These are hypotheses. If you join the waitlist, tell us what would make it worth it, or not.

## What it is not

- **The free tool does the same checks.** Upkeep runs them for you, keeps the history and writes the report. Penthera stays free and MIT, and nothing is taken out of it.
- **Your source code is never uploaded.** Code checks run in your own CI; Upkeep only reads their results.
- **Not a certification and not a pentest.** It shows what was checked and what was found, with the evidence.

## How the waitlist works

The button opens an email to upkeep@penthera.org with three lines to fill in: company, team size and role. Nothing is stored on penthera.org. We reply to every message, and we only write again when there is something to try.
